A VERY SHORT INSTRUCTION MANUAL

Make a killing.
Not literally.

You have eight nights to survive the auction house's ever-growing dues. Buy oddities, arrange them in your cabinet, and sell them to whoever visits tonight.

  1. BUY up to two relics from the Back Room each night. Relics arrive in the first empty space.
  2. ARRANGE your relics: select one, then tap an empty slot to move it. Neighbours and positions increase their value.
  3. TAG the relics you want in tonight's auction using the small + LOT label. Relics you keep can still boost the ones you sell.
  4. SELL one lot per night. Each buyer pays 50% more for their preferred relic type. Two matching relics earn a £12 collection bonus.
  5. END NIGHT to pay your dues. Unsold items may become cursed and eventually shatter. Every relic shows its individual curse risk when inspected.

Tip: A clever cabinet can be more valuable than a full one. Look for combinations, and keep an eye on tomorrow's buyer.
